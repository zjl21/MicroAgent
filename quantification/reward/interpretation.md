## Parameter Map Quality Report Interpretation Guide

- `R2`: structural fidelity; higher is better.
- `MAE` / `rel_MAE`: absolute and relative parameter error; lower is better.
- `Bias`: signed systematic error; closer to 0 is better.
- `pos_ratio`: fraction of voxels with Pred > GT; closer to 0.5 is better.
- `mean_angular_error_deg` / `median_angular_error_deg`: V1 direction error; lower is better.
